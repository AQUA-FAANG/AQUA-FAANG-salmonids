# Supplemetary data used in the manuscript "Salmonids reveal principles of regulatory evolution following autotetraploidization" (Baudement, Perojil Morata, Gillard et al.; corresponding authors Daniel J. Macqueen and Sigbjørn Lien).

**Supplementary Data 1.** MultiQC reports spanning all sequencing data produced in the study.

**Supplementary Data 2.** Genomic coordinates of 225,801 reference robust open chromatin regions in the Atlantic salmon genome, including coordinates of their syntenic/orthologous regions within the Atlantic salmon, rainbow trout and northern pike genomes, based on whole genome alignment.

**Supplementary Data 3.** Final dataset of active promoter and enhancer elements belonging to the three Alignment categories (‘Shared’, ‘Alignable only’ and ‘Exclusive only’)

**Supplementary Data 4.** All conserved CNEs defined in the northern pike genome, including their estimated phylogenetic age.

**Supplementary Data 5.** Atlantic salmon enhancer-CNEs from the three alignment categories: ‘Shared’, ‘Alignable only’ and ‘Exclusive’.

**Supplementary Data 6.** Atlantic salmon enhancer-CNEs counts and length information split per: sample-types), ii) alignment category (Shared, Alignable only, Exclusive) and iii) rediploidization region (Early and Late).
